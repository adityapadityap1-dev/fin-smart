FIN SMART — GOVERNMENT SCHEME DIRECTORY SOURCES
================================================

The in-app scheme directory is an educational overview. Always confirm current
eligibility, benefits, contribution/premium, documents and application process
on the official scheme website before acting.

1. PM Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
   https://pmjdy.gov.in/scheme
   Department of Financial Services:
   https://www.financialservices.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-jan-dhan-yojana-pmjdy

2. PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
   https://financialservices.gov.in/financial-inclusion-fi

3. PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
   https://financialservices.gov.in/financial-inclusion-fi

4. Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
   https://www.pfrda.org.in/
   Department of Financial Services scheme list:
   https://financialservices.gov.in/schemes-and-services

5. PM-Vidyalaxmi
   https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in/
   Ministry of Education scheme information:
   https://www.education.gov.in/

6. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
   https://www.mudra.org.in/

7. myScheme — Government of India scheme discovery portal
   https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

General financial inclusion scheme information:
https://financialservices.gov.in/schemes-and-services

This directory does not submit applications, verify an individual's eligibility,
or represent the Government of India. Scheme details and official URLs may change.
