Protect your money

Financial Safety Centre

Learn to recognise common digital-payment and investment scams. Fin Smart will never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or full card details.

UPI safety

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money

A UPI PIN authorises a payment from your account. Check the direction and amount before approving any request.

Phishing

Do not trust urgent links

Verify messages through the bank's official app or website. Do not install remote-access apps at the request of unknown callers.

Investments

Be sceptical of guaranteed high returns

Promises of assured extraordinary profits, pressure to act quickly and unregistered advisers are warning signs.

OTP fraud

Keep OTPs and passwords private

Do not share OTPs, card PINs, CVV, passwords or screen-sharing access with anyone claiming to help.

Loans

Check lenders and fees

Verify the regulated lender, total repayment, fees, privacy terms and grievance channel before accepting a loan.

Account security

Act quickly if you suspect fraud

Contact your bank immediately and use India's official cybercrime reporting channels.

National Cyber Crime Portal ↗Reserve Bank of India ↗SEBI ↗
If money has been lost to cyber fraud in India, contact your bank immediately and call the national cyber fraud helpline 1930 as soon as possible. Use official channels and retain transaction details.
